About

Architectural professional · Curator · Speaker · Writer

Working where ideas and spaces meet.

Thobani Ndlovu is a South African architectural professional, curator, speaker and writer whose work explores how spaces and ideas shape people, communities and cities.

Through Kumana, he works across architecture and spatial design. As Curator and Licensee of TEDxDurban, he builds platforms for ideas, develops speakers and creates rooms in which Durban can think aloud about its future.

These two worlds are connected by one enduring interest: the environments we create, and the people we become within them.

Background
National Diploma in Architectural Technology · Advanced Diploma in Business Administration · Postgraduate studies in Project Management
